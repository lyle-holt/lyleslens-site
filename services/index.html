Services

Services and prices

What Lyle's Lens offers, and what it costs. Prices carry no GST because Lyle's Lens isn't registered for it.

Sport and event coverage

Matches, rounds, finals and events. From $550 for up to two hours, $1,000 for a half day and $1,850 for a full day. Edited selection within 48 hours.

Media days

Studio-lit portraits for a squad or a whole league in one day. Shot to your brief from $1,850 for up to about 70 athletes. A day planned as a season library is quoted separately.

Athlete identity

For athletes and their families. $650 for an hour in the studio with five retouched images, or $1,200 with training or action added and ten images.

Executive portraits

For leaders, boards and speakers. On-location headshots from $550, portrait sessions $950, a studio portrait library $1,600, and team headshots.

Discovery Audit

A written review of the photos your organisation already has, against what you need them to do. $1,200, credited against any work that follows.

Ongoing arrangements

Season and annual arrangements for organisations, priced below one-off rates in return for regular work. By proposal.

Add-ons: drone stills and video $275 a session, subject to weather, venue permission and CASA rules. Short social video edits from $350. Travel beyond Canberra at cost.
