For sporting organisations

Start with an audit, or book a shoot directly. See how it works →

1. Start with what you have

Discovery Audit

A written review of the photos your organisation already has, against what you need them to do. $1,200, credited against any work that follows.

2. Build what is missing

Sport and event coverage

Matches, rounds, finals and events. From $550 for up to two hours, $1,000 for a half day and $1,850 for a full day. Edited selection within 48 hours.

Teams and media days

Teams of up to 16 are $300 plus $95 a person, with two retouched photographs each and individual direction for every person. A team of eight is $1,060 and a team of twelve is $1,440.

For larger groups, Lyle provides high volume portrait shoots that create a consistent set of photographs across your group. As a guide, $1,850 covers up to 100 people, with three retouched photographs per person to give you variety for content across your channels throughout the season or year. It uses agreed poses, one location, one setup and one delivery format, with people attending to an agreed running order. Larger teams and additional requirements are quoted to your brief. The difference is direction: team portraits give each person individual direction, while a high-volume day uses agreed poses, one setup and an agreed running order. Team portraits are also available to businesses and boards, booked directly.

Aerial and video

Drone stills and video, $275 per hour, subject to weather, venue permission and CASA rules. The scope, deliverables, editing and total price are confirmed in your quote before booking. Short social video edits from $350. See the aerial and documentation examples, including Fleet Helicopters (a workplace and its operational assets) and the Invictus Australia sport expo (an event and the people taking part). Video from the AFL Canberra media day and Chill v Blaze match day is on the sports pages.

3. Keep it useful

Ongoing arrangements

Season and annual arrangements that keep your library current, including regular match coverage. Priced below one-off rates and agreed around your schedule. By agreement.

Portraits for people

For athletes, leaders and speakers. Lyle plans your portraits around how you will use them. Studio lighting, retouching and a usage licence are included. Choose a straightforward headshot, a broader range of portraits or an identity library.

Headshot, $550

Four photographs of your choice. One look on one background, for your profiles, your website and announcements.

Profile portraits, $950

Eight photographs of your choice. Two looks and an agreed background, for more variety across your profiles, the media and your website.

Identity library, $1,600

Twelve photographs of your choice, in up to six looks. Studio backgrounds are included. So are environmental photographs, made at your location during the session or by placing a studio-lit photograph of you into an agreed setting, and they count within your twelve. Each photograph is prepared in six formats for use across multiple channels. Masters are kept for three years, so new crops and backgrounds can be made later without a reshoot.

A look is a change of clothing. Lyle brings studio lighting to you: sessions at your workplace, home, club, training ground or another agreed location are included. Outdoor shoots are quoted before booking. Settings that need separate photography or a particular time of day are quoted separately. An additional photograph is $150, within your session’s looks and backgrounds.

Delivery: Portraits are normally delivered within five business days of your final image selection. Larger or more complex jobs may take longer. Your delivery date is confirmed before booking.

Add-ons for Profile portraits and the identity library: Ambassador and speaking portraits $450, Brand or team colours $350 (library only), Additional location $300, and Bespoke setting, quoted. Hair and make-up at cost, and delivery within 48 hours of choosing your photographs for $150, subject to availability confirmed before booking.
