Athlete Photography

Athlete photography and identity

Athletes shouldn't have to wait until they're established before anyone starts building a photo library around them.

An organisation invests heavily in an athlete's physical potential long before it invests in her commercial potential.

Start early, so the images are there when she needs them.

Photograph her while she’s still coming through, and the images are ready when opportunities come up.

The same set of images covers three needs. The programme has evidence to put in front of a board or funder. The partner has something it can actually run. The athlete has an identity that works for her now and goes with her as she moves up.

Each image is planned for where a board, funder or sponsor will see it: an annual report, a funding submission, a sponsor deck or a media release.

Pricing

Athlete portraits.

If you are under 18, a parent or guardian books and signs for you. Every package includes studio lighting, retouching and permission to use your photographs (see Using your photos below). You choose your favourite photographs from a preview gallery.

Headshot, $550. Four photographs in one outfit, on one background. A clean set for your social media, your bio, your club and the media, and for introducing yourself to possible sponsors.

Profile portraits, $950. Eight photographs in two outfits, with a background agreed before the shoot. More choice for your social media, your club and the media, and your sponsor introductions.

Identity library, $1,600. Twelve photographs in up to six outfits, with backgrounds agreed before the shoot. A varied set to keep using as you move up, whenever you need photographs for your social media, your bio, your club, the media or a sponsor introduction. Each one comes in six sizes for profile pictures, social posts, stories and banners. Lyle keeps the files for three years, so new crops or backgrounds can be made later. Extra editing is quoted separately.

Outfits can be your playing kit, your training gear or your own clothes. Sessions can take place at your training ground, gym or clubhouse, or in the studio, with the location agreed before booking. Other people are usually around, and Lyle can often photograph you just before or after training. You are welcome to bring a parent, guardian or support person. Sessions at these places are included. A second location, such as the pitch or a stadium at night, is $300, and a session held entirely outdoors is quoted before booking. An additional photograph is $150, in the outfits and backgrounds you have already chosen.

Using your photos

Licence

What you can do. Use your photographs on your own social media and personal website, in your bio, when you get in touch with a possible sponsor, and when you apply for selection, pathways and scholarships. You can also give them to your club or a journalist to publish, with a photo credit to Lyle’s Lens Photography.

If your club or programme books the session. It can also use the photographs on its website, social media, publicity, reports and funding applications.

If a sponsor wants to use them. If a sponsor wants to use your photographs in its own advertising or on its own social media, including reposting yours, contact Lyle to arrange a sponsor licence. It sets out where the photographs can appear and for how long, usually 12 months. Television and billboard advertising are quoted separately. Your club or programme can help arrange this. Lyle also needs your agreement, and a parent or guardian’s if you are under 18.

Your say. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian signs the release, and it stays in force. From 18, you decide on any new commercial use. AI training and computer-made copies of you are never included and need your separate written consent.

A 50% deposit books the session, with the balance due on delivery of your selected photographs. You can reschedule once for free with 48 hours’ notice. Inside 48 hours, the deposit isn’t refundable.
