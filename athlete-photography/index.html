Athlete Photography & Identity

Athletes shouldn't have to wait until they are established before the visual infrastructure around them begins.

An organisation invests heavily in an athlete's physical potential long before it invests in her commercial potential.

Build it early and it's worth more.

The programmes that build the assets around her while she is still emerging capture more of the value that arrives later.

One asset base, built once, returns three ways: the programme gets evidence it can put in front of a board or a funder, the partner gets something it can actually run, and the athlete gets an identity that works for her now and travels with her as she progresses.

Every frame is built to answer the questions a board, a funder, or a sponsor is about to ask in an annual report, a funding submission, a sponsor deck, a media release.
