Case Record 01

Hockey ACT — Athlete Identity Programme

The Problem

Emerging elite athletes in Canberra are tracking toward national pathway selection and state representation, often training at a full load while also working to support themselves.1 As global women's sport revenue is projected to hit US$3 billion in 2026, up 25% in a year,2 elite programmes elsewhere in Australia — including the AFL — back their athletes' trajectory with decision-grade visual evidence that strengthens the case in board papers, funding submissions and sponsor conversations.3 Canberra programmes are already riding that growth; my framework is what capturing more of it, for the organisation and the athlete alike, looks like.

The Gap

My assessment: existing social media coverage documented what happened on the field — not the programme behind it. Missing was a standardised identity layer for a selection case, documentation built for governance and funding audiences, and a values-aligned narrative connecting on-field performance to community, culture and wellbeing — the layers this architecture was built to close.

The Architecture

A centralised, modular asset library built across four layers: standardised identity portraits for selection and credentialing; Pride Round and Reconciliation Round coverage for community and values reporting; and Livin for Hockey coverage for athlete wellbeing and culture. One production, engineered once, deployable across every use case below.

The Result

Decision-grade imagery, delivered with full release compliance and ready to deploy the moment it arrives. And because it was architected rather than just shot, the same library is now doing work well outside its original brief — see below.

Case Record 02

AFL Canberra — Season Media Day

Context

AFL Canberra needed consistent, professional imagery across roughly 70 athletes from multiple clubs before the season began.

Strategy

A single structured media day producing around 200 studio-lit identity portraits as a season-long asset library — not event coverage.

Method

Lighting design engineered to capture both athlete faces and sponsor marks clearly across every channel.

Operational Value

One production day yielded approximately 200 deployable assets with consistent visual standards across all clubs.

Case Record 04

American Football ACT's Flag Football competition

Context

American Football ACT's flag football competition, February to June 2024. Four months of coverage across multiple venues, provided pro bono, as the code built depth toward flag football's debut as a full medal sport at the Los Angeles 2028 Olympic Games.

Strategy

Weekly delivery engineered for the league's own channels — recruitment, community building and social deployment — with scalability to commercial use.

Operational Value

Imagery deployed across the league's social channels throughout the season. A full 2024 tackle season was subsequently scoped with the league — fourteen weeks, four men's teams, three women's, junior and state fixtures — before the arrangement lapsed.
