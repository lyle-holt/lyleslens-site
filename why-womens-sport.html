Our Proving Ground

Why women's elite sport.

Women's elite sport in Australia is not an emerging market. It is an underbuilt one. The athlete quality is there. The funding pressure is there. The sponsor scrutiny is arriving. What is missing is the visual evidence.

The Participation Gap

Half of all girls leave sport before they finish school.

Dropout rates run to 60 per cent for girls aged 15 and over, compared with 42 per cent for boys. By the age of 17, half of all Australian girls had left sport entirely, despite two-thirds saying it made them feel more confident. The reasons cited: body image, social pressure, competition structure, and a lack of visible role models.

The Role Model Effect

Girls stay when they can see someone to become.

60 per cent of young Australian women had no female sports star they looked up to. AFLW participation grew by 21,000 in a single year, reaching 176,000 players. A UK study found 66 per cent of girls attributed their interest in sport to female athletes, and Toronto research shows visible role models reduce dropout.

The Commercial Case

The fans are worth more. The infrastructure is missing.

Women's sport fans watch for longer, spend more, and show three times the brand affinity of the average sports fan. Yet the infrastructure gap between established men's programmes and emerging women's ones remains wide.

The Just Cause

A world where women athletes are fully visible.

Because the value they create does not end at the final whistle.
