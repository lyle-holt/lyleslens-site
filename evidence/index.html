The Evidence

The Evidence Record.

Lyle's Lens makes sports photography and image libraries for sporting organisations, pathway programmes and their partners. The photos are planned around three questions a board or partner will ask. Can this programme represent us? Can we justify funding it? Will this partnership stand up when the board reviews it for renewal?

The case records below show how that works in practice.

One photograph, planned for three different jobs.
