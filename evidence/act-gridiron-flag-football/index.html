American Football ACT's flag football competition

Context

American Football ACT's flag football competition, February to June 2024. Lyle covered four months of games across several venues. The code was building depth ahead of flag football's debut as a full medal sport at the Los Angeles 2028 Olympic Games.

Strategy

Photos were delivered weekly for the league's own channels, to help with recruitment, community building and social media. They could also be scaled up for commercial use.
