Case Record 04

Cross-sector work

Lyle uses the same approach outside elite sport. This page covers two examples. One is a board appointment portrait session for a director of an ASX-listed defence and space company. The other is a US Department of Defense school athletics programme. The method works anywhere a decision-maker needs photos they can stand behind, from a hockey pitch to a boardroom.

Board appointment portrait

Behind the scenes at Lyle’s portrait session for Air Vice-Marshal (Ret’d) Catherine Roberts AO, CSC, for the announcement of her appointment to the board of ASX-listed Electro Optic Systems.1 The image EOS published was a version altered after delivery, so it isn’t shown here as an example of Lyle’s photography.
