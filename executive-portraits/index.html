Portraits for leaders

Executive portraits

Portraits for CEOs, board members, senior officers and speakers. For LinkedIn, board and speaker bios, company announcements and the media.

Lit properly, and cropped for where you'll use it.

Lyle photographs leaders the way he photographs athletes, with proper lighting and clear direction on the day. He'll come to your office or a location that suits, or you can come to the studio.

Every image arrives ready to use. The larger sessions include a six-format set sized for LinkedIn, Instagram, presentations and your website, so nobody has to resize anything later.

Black backgrounds suit board, legal and finance profiles. White sits cleanly on websites and LinkedIn headers. The studio library includes both.

Using your photos

Licence and booking

Use your images for your own professional profile anywhere, for as long as you like. When your employer or an agency books the session, the licence covers company use too: website, announcements, annual report and media. Advertising is licensed separately.

A 50% deposit secures the booking, with the balance due on delivery. You can reschedule once for free with 48 hours' notice.
