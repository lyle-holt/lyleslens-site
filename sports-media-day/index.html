AFL Canberra: Season Media Day

A media day should produce more than social content.

There are two ways to run a media day. Lyle can shoot to your brief: AFL Canberra wanted 5x4 portraits on a white background, so their media team could cut the athletes out and place them on their own designs. That’s the simpler, cheaper option.

Or the day can be planned as a library you’ll keep using, with each portrait framed with room for copy and cropped for every channel, ready for partner and sponsor material all season.

A partner who has paid for a logo but has nothing to run in its own advertising has a problem, with budget already committed to it. Media day images give that partner something to use, and give you material for the renewal conversation.

Lyle’s Lens counts a photo as finished when the people making the decision can drop it straight into the document, deck or report in front of them.

Pricing

Media days.

For larger groups, Lyle provides high volume portrait shoots that create a consistent set of photographs across your group. As a guide, $1,850 covers up to 100 people, with three retouched photographs per person to give you variety for content across your channels throughout the season or year. It uses agreed poses, one location, one setup and one delivery format, with people attending to an agreed running order. Larger groups and additional requirements are quoted to your brief.
