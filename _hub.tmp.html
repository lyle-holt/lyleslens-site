The Evidence

The Evidence Architecture.

Lyle's Lens produces sports photography and visual asset systems for sporting organisations, pathway programmes and institutional partners. Our media asset framework resolves the three strategic questions that dictate institutional and commercial support: can this programme represent us, can it justify our funding support, and can this partnership withstand scrutiny as valuable, defensible, and renewable at board level?

Visible. Credible. Investable. The case records below show the framework applied.

One Production. Three Deployments.

The difference isn't the photograph. It's what the photograph was built to do.

One frame, shot once, from a Hockey ACT production — engineered with deliberate negative space rather than cropped tight to the action. Below is how that single photograph is prepared for three different institutional purposes. Two are shown applied to mock deployments — a report cover and a sponsor banner — built with fictional placeholder names ("Capital Region Sports Trust," "Meridian Health Partners") for illustration only. These are not real clients, and none of this is published or commissioned work.
