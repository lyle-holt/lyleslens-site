What your photos need to do

Can your photos do these six jobs?

Most sporting organisations have game-day coverage, media-day portraits and years of social content. What's often missing is the photo a sponsor, a board or a new recruit actually needs.

Recruitment

Show prospective athletes what the pathway looks like.

Funding

Show funders what their money supports.

Communications

Pathways

Show athletes developing and the programme behind them.

Sponsorship

Governance

How it works

Start with what you already have.

Before anyone picks up a camera, Lyle reviews the photos your organisation already owns against the jobs you need them to do. The Discovery Audit shows what already works, what can be reused, where the gaps are, and whether a new shoot is worth it. You share the photos you already have and tell Lyle what they need to do, and you get his findings in writing. It costs $1,200, credited against any work that follows.

Already know what you need? Book a shoot directly. No audit required.
