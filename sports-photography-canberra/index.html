Sports photography for Canberra's sporting organisations

Lyle's Lens is a Canberra-based elite sports photography and photo library practice, working with the organisations building elite pathways.

Visual evidence built around what your organisation needs to show, ready for when you have to show it.

You probably have thousands of photographs of what happened. When a board, funder or sponsor asks you to show the programme’s value, can you use any of them in the case for continued funding or a partnership renewal?

Lyle’s Lens counts a photo as finished when the people making the decision can drop it straight into the document, deck or report in front of them.
