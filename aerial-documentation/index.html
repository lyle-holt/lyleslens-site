Services

Aerial photography and documentation

Drone and ground photography and video that records a site, a business or an event as it stands, so you have current media for your website, capability statements and tenders, recruitment, and a dated record of your buildings and equipment.

Lyle flies a DJI Mavic 3 Pro under a CASA Remote Operator Certificate (ReOC) and Remote Pilot Licence (RePL). Each job starts with what the media needs to show and how you’ll use it, then the flights and ground photography are planned around that, subject to weather, site permission and CASA rules. Prices are on the Services page.

Invictus Australia sport expo → An event and the people taking part, on the sports photography page.
Fleet Helicopters, Armidale Regional Airport, August 2025. Lyle spent two days at the base so the business had a current, usable record of its hangars, aircraft and equipment. The photographs and video are intended for the operator’s website and social media, capability statements and tenders, recruitment, and as a dated record of its aircraft and equipment. Music: Sam Will, “The Long Ride”, courtesy of Epidemic Sound.
Silos Estate, Berry, New South Wales, June 2025. A sunrise flight over the vineyard and dams, around the converted silo buildings and visitor facilities, then out to the farmland and Coolangatta Mountain. It records how the property is laid out and how it sits in its setting, which suits a property’s website, listings and promotional material. Music: Sunfish Grove, “Unbroken Spirit”, courtesy of Epidemic Sound.
Aerial documentation of a farm property in Canterbury, New Zealand: the layout of the site, its roofs and structures inside and out, and how they sit in the wider property. The same approach can document sporting grounds, stadium roofs and lighting infrastructure. Music: Out To The World, “Motion Clarity”, courtesy of Epidemic Sound.
Bangalay Luxury Villas, Shoalhaven Heads, New South Wales, June 2025. Dawn on Seven Mile Beach, then the villas, pools and gardens, a flight into a villa, Bangalay Dining and the golf course, back to the coast, and finishing on the estuary and the beach. It shows a venue’s accommodation and amenities and where they sit between the ocean and the golf course. Music: Jakob Ahlbom, “Crossing the Rubicon”, courtesy of Epidemic Sound.
Outback Air Race 2025, August and September 2025. Lyle took part with Team Charlie A. Foxtrot, a fundraiser for the Royal Flying Doctor Service, and filmed the journey from inside the aircraft: the departure from Nowra, the flight over Mundi Mundi and Kati Thanda–Lake Eyre, remote outback stops, the race fleet from the drone at Onslow and the RFDS at Broken Hill. Music: Adriel Fair, “Ropes Away”, courtesy of Epidemic Sound.
Harvest on a cropping farm in Canterbury, New Zealand, summer 2024–25. Lyle’s own aerial film, shot on his DJI Mavic 3 Pro. Music: Bonnie Grace, “The Sound of Arrows”, courtesy of Epidemic Sound.
