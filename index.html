Operational Standards

Built to institutional standard

Rights and Releases

All imagery delivered rights-cleared with signed talent releases. Usage rights, licensing scope and any territory or duration conditions are agreed before production begins.

Delivery Format

Full-resolution, social-media crops and print-ready files delivered as standard. Modular crops across multiple aspect ratios are included in every engagement.

Insurance and Clearances

Professional indemnity and public liability insurance held. CASA ReOC and RePL certified for aerial operations. ACT Working with Vulnerable People registered.

Archive Access

Client archives maintained on a live delivery platform for a minimum of three years. Assets can be re-accessed or re-ordered after the engagement is complete.
