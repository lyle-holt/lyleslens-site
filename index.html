How an Engagement Works

Diagnosis first. Production only where it is justified.

No cameras. The Discovery Audit produces a written gap analysis and structural recommendations report, built to hold up under scrutiny.

That discipline isn't borrowed from photography. It's the same judgement built over thirty-four years identifying what mattered under pressure, working from incomplete information, and giving people a clear recommendation they could act on — now aimed at your visual evidence.

Each audit is scoped and priced to the organisation.

01

Your trigger

An annual report. A funding submission. A sponsorship renewal. A sponsor with nothing to activate. A selection window. A recruitment problem. A content calendar that never stops.

Different pressures. One question underneath them: Is your visual evidence strong enough to carry the decision?

02

Discovery Audit

We start with the visual assets you already hold.

We review your existing library against the requirements of the people who make funding decisions — boards, funders, selectors, and your own partners. What could each partner actually run? What have they asked for and not received? Where does the next renewal get harder?

03

Report and recommendations

You receive a written gap analysis and structural recommendations report on the health of your current library. It stands on its own.

It also gives you something to take to your partners: a specific case for what closing those gaps is worth to the people already funding your programme.

What happens next is your decision. There is no production commitment.
