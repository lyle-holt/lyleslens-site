Why women's sport

Why women's elite sport.

Most of the support that turns an athlete’s ability into recognition (media profile, identity, sponsor relationships) arrives once she reaches the top.

Lyle works with women’s pathways to bring some of that forward. Building the athlete’s and the programme’s identity while they’re still developing means their value can be seen and used earlier.

The pathway is full of genuinely competitive sport: state leagues, national championships and finals with selection on the line. That creates opportunities for a different set of brands, particularly local and regional businesses that want to be connected to the clubs and athletes in their community. It’s a different market from the elite game, and a real one.

Sponsors get more out of it too: photos of their brand with the athletes and moments their community follows, ready to use in their own channels.

Start early, and the photos grow with the athlete and the programme.
