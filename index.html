Standards

What every client gets

Rights and releases

Signed releases for everyone in frame. Usage rights, scope and any time or territory limits are agreed before the shoot.

Files

Full-resolution, social and print-ready files as standard, with crops in several aspect ratios.

Insurance and accreditation

Professional indemnity and public liability insurance. CASA ReOC and RePL for drone work. ACT Working with Vulnerable People registration. Hockey One League media accreditation at all grounds in Australia.

Archive

Your files stay on a live delivery platform for at least three years, so you can come back for them.
